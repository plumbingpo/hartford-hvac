Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched fast, anywhere in Hartford and the surrounding area.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses
